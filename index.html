A bit about me, and how I build

I'm Daniel Ander, a Product Manager focused on 0→1 discovery, product strategy, and turning rough ideas into products people actually use.

Who I am

Most of my time goes into talking with users, figuring out what's actually broken, and working closely with engineers and designers to ship things that matter.

More about me

Talk to users first

Good product decisions rarely happen in a vacuum. I spend time with real users to uncover the real problem, then bring what I learn straight back to the team.

Test early, before scaling

Instead of guessing for months, I put a rough prototype in front of people early, see where they get stuck, and adjust fast.
