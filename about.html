Principles

How I Work

A few simple habits I try to stick to on every product team.

01

Talk to users first

Good product decisions rarely happen in a vacuum. I spend time talking to real users to understand their actual problems, then bring what I learn straight back to the team.

02

Test early, before committing

Instead of guessing for months, I'd rather put a rough prototype in front of people, see where they get stuck, and adjust fast.

03

Outcomes over busywork

Shipping features is easy; shipping something that actually changes how someone works or solves a real headache is the hard part. That's what I optimize for.

Product Discovery

Four Questions I Ask Before Scaling

Before a team spends weeks building, I like to pressure-test an idea against four plain questions.

Value

Will people actually want and use this?

Usability

Can someone figure it out without a manual?

Feasibility

Can our engineering team build it reliably with what we have?

Viability

Does it work for the business, operations, and compliance?
