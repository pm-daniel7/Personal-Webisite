MoneyFlow

Local-first multi-currency cashflow sync.

Built for personal use (not a public product).

The Problem

Why spreadsheets break under daily cashflow friction

Spreadsheets start with high intent, but quickly break under the friction of daily life. Between micro cash spends, multi-currency income, and reimbursable work expenses, manual tracking becomes exhausting, leading to tracking fatigue, stale mid-month spreadsheets, and silent formula errors.

Core Frictions

Four failure modes I needed to eliminate

01

Logging Fatigue

Spending 30–60 seconds per small transaction across multiple tabs causes habit drop-off within weeks.

02

Fragile Spreadsheet Formulas

Manually carrying balances forward (Auto BBF), deducting work expenses, and tracking peer loans leads to calculation bugs.

03

Multi-Currency & FX Distortion

Earning across USD, GBP, or EUR breaks single-currency trackers while still requiring unified Naira (₦) visibility.

04

SaaS Lock-In vs. Data Privacy

Commercial budgeting tools store sensitive financial history on third-party servers, while offline-only tools trap records without backup.

The Prototype

Logged in < 3 seconds, synced to my private Google Sheet

A personal, zero-SaaS offline PWA that records expenses in under 3 seconds and syncs directly to my private Google Sheet for reporting.
