[STRATEGIC VISION]

Driving customer-centric innovation and product strategy from discovery to scale. Dedicated to turning complex market challenges into intuitive, scalable products that deliver measurable customer impact and compound business growth.

[WHAT I BELIEVE]

  • ↳ CONTINUOUS DISCOVERY & DELIVERY: Going out there to talk to users, uncovering the real problem, and feeding that directly into what we build.
  • ↳ RAPID EXPERIMENTATION: A continuous loop of prototyping and testing with real users, validating assumptions early, and shipping what actually works.
  • ↳ OUTCOMES OVER OUTPUTS: It's not just about building and shipping features, but about the measurable customer outcomes and compounding value of what we ship.

[PRODUCT OPERATING PHILOSOPHY]

Rooted in the SVPG product discovery and management principles championed by Marty Cagan, Teresa Torres, and Shreyas Doshi. Systematically evaluating and de-risking the four big product risks before scaling:

  • ↳ VALUE RISK: Will customers choose to use and buy this product?
  • ↳ USABILITY RISK: Can users easily figure out how to navigate and use it?
  • ↳ FEASIBILITY RISK: Can our engineering and tech team deliver it within constraints?
  • ↳ VIABILITY RISK: Does this product work for our business, legal, and operational strategy?
