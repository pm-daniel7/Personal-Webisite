MONEYFLOW [PERSONAL · PROTOTYPING PHASE]

LOCAL-FIRST MULTI-CURRENCY CASHFLOW SYNC.

BUILT FOR PERSONAL USE (NOT A PUBLIC PRODUCT).

[THE PROBLEM]

Spreadsheets start with high intent, but quickly break under the friction of daily life. Between micro cash spends, multi-currency income, and reimbursable work expenses, manual tracking becomes exhausting—leading to tracking fatigue, stale mid-month spreadsheets, and silent formula errors.

[CORE FRICTIONS]

  • ↳ LOGGING FATIGUE: Spending 30–60 seconds per small transaction across multiple tabs causes habit drop-off within weeks.
  • ↳ FRAGILE SPREADSHEET FORMULAS: Manually carrying balances forward (Auto BBF), deducting work expenses, and tracking peer loans leads to calculation bugs.
  • ↳ MULTI-CURRENCY & FX DISTORTION: Earning across USD, GBP, or EUR breaks single-currency trackers while still requiring unified Naira (₦) visibility.
  • ↳ SAAS LOCK-IN VS. DATA PRIVACY: Commercial budgeting tools store sensitive financial history on third-party servers, while offline-only tools trap records without backup.

[THE PROTOTYPE]

A personal, zero-SaaS offline PWA that records expenses in under 3 seconds and syncs directly to my private Google Sheet for reporting.
